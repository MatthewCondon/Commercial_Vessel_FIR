# GPX File Viewer
## Summary
This is an online tool that maps coordinates that a vessel passed during a voyage. This is effective to see surrounding locations to a voyage track that may have impacted a vessel.

<p align="center">
  <img src="/Images/gpxview.png" alt="GPX VIEW Tool" width="100%">
</p>

## Requirements
**.gpx** file

This may be collected from some VDR models or produced/converted from analysis. GPX requires three columns: time, latitude, and longitude. Additional data may be inserted into the file, as VIEWGPX also tracks elevation and speed.
