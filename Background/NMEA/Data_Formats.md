# NMEA 0183
NMEA 0183 is an older maritime communication standard. While it has largely been replaced by NMEA 2000, ships often still employ NMEA 0183 due to their age and associated devices.

The format varies by device, but generally the format looks similar to the data below:
```
2026-9-8 13:00:00 UTC | $ERALR,130103.52,C04,A,A,NORMAL*3E
2026-9-8 13:00:00 UTC | $ERALR,124154.62,C14,A,V,NORMAL*20
2026-9-8 13:00:00 UTC | $ERALR,010335.94,L11,A,A,NORMAL*23
2026-9-8 13:00:00 UTC | $GPRMB,A,5.19,L,WPT001,WPT002,4006.4952,N,07403.4727,W,008.3,178,002.3,V*6B
2026-9-8 13:00:00 UTC | $GPZTG,124327,,*4F
2026-9-8 13:00:00 UTC | $GPGGA,124328,4006.4952,N,07403.4727,W,1,04,2.5,0010,M,-033,M,,*51
```

# NMEA 2000
NMEA 2000 is the newer communication standard and is used on vessels that have been produced recently. Typically, there may still be NMEA 0183 available on devices. This is because some vessels employ older equipment.

There are three relevant formats for analysis:
